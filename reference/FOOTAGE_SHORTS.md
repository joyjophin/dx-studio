# Footage and talking-head videos

Use recordings the creator supplied or media they have permission to redistribute.
`scripts/footage.py --help` lists extraction, grid and letter-reveal utilities.
Use contact sheets to identify exact events; measure crop coordinates from frames.
Keep original files in project assets so the editor can rebuild the result.

A vertical cut from landscape footage needs a deliberate crop or a staged card;
do not stretch the image. Pan only when tracking an actual moving subject. A
release video is evidence of visible behaviour, not permission to invent features.
Label recreated interfaces and verify current product behaviour when necessary.

For a talking-head version, agree on the creator's footage region. A useful
portrait starting layout places graphics in the upper 60% with room below for
the speaker. Put graphics, captions and the camera crop in separate layers.
Change the layout for landscape rather than applying the same percentages blindly.

DX Editor's + Media imports images, GIFs and footage. Uploaded videos are decoded
and cached locally; their audio can be muted or mixed. Media decoders require
FFmpeg and ffprobe. Keep source footage and saved media settings in project bundles.
