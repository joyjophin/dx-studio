# Teaching explainers

Use a chapter structure when several lessons advance one central question.
Choose length from the topic, not a fixed list size or six-minute requirement.

For each chapter: demonstrate a concrete failure, explain its cause, apply a fix,
and show the corrected result under comparable conditions. Keep one consistent
fictional example product when it makes changes easier to compare. Label fictional
measurements and do not present them as research. Cite factual claims in a source list.

`kits/teaching16x9.py` offers optional devices, UI controls, annotations, contrast
measurements and chapter helpers. Copy the kit beside scene.py and adapt its
palette and labels. The kit is an illustrative drawing library; verify product
behaviour separately. Personal example productions are not part of this release.

Use chapters and clear transitions, occasional recap frames, and visual proof.
Memes and stock imagery are optional creator-supplied assets with their own rights.
Keep captions editable; long videos may ship SRT instead of burned captions.
Deliver source notes, voice lines and timings, editable project and MP4.
